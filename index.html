Create a new video from an idea or script

EASIEST FIRST TRY

Image story · Fastest

Result: AI pictures with pan and zoom, plus your selected narration and subtitles. Characters within the pictures do not move.

Example: “Explain the water cycle” becomes a narrated sequence of clouds, rain and rivers.

Needs: A connected planner and image service. Enable a narration service if you want a voiceover.

MOTION OPENING

Hybrid · 1 motion shot + images

Result: An AI motion opening followed by pictures with pan and zoom. Narration and subtitles follow your selections.

Example: A moving car in the opening shot, followed by images explaining its features.

Needs: A planner, image service and Colab GPU with motion generation enabled. Usually takes longer than Image story.

ALL SHOTS MOVE

Full AI motion · Slowest

Result: Generated movement in every scene, with optional narration and subtitles. Exact actions and consistent character appearance are not guaranteed.

Example: A robot walks into a room → turns toward a window → walks outside.

Needs: A connected planner and Colab GPU with motion generation enabled. This is the slowest option; start with a short video.

TEACH / EXPLAIN

Avatar explainer · Slides + voice

Result: A presenter alongside topic slides and narration. For example, “Explain photosynthesis” becomes a presenter-led lesson with illustrated slides.

Illustrated · Fast: A drawn presenter with speech-driven mouth animation, blinks and gestures. Requires a planner, image service and narration service.

AI talking avatar · Colab: An AI-generated face whose mouth follows the narration. It does not generate head or body gestures. Requires additional presenter setup and narration. Run Check setup → Test 5 seconds first.

Your AI video, step by step

  1. Connect & Test: Run the SmartEdit Colab notebook and paste its current ONE-PASTE CONNECTION LINK. Wait for the required services to become ready. Keep Colab running during generation.
  2. Idea + settings: Enter a topic or finished script. Choose the video type, language, format, target duration, narration and subtitles.
  3. Plan scenes (optional): Create an editable storyboard and review the scene text and order. This step does not produce a finished MP4.
  4. Draft video preview (optional): Create a 720p MP4 to check pacing, voice and subtitles. Hybrid and Full AI motion drafts use images, so they do not preview the final movement. Avatar drafts use your selected presenter and still require its setup.
  5. Generate video: Produce a video in the selected mode. The app also plans scenes if you have not already done so. Follow the progress panel; completion time depends on your GPU, connection, duration and mode.
  6. Review → Save → Export → Download: Preview or download the generated video. If you edit its timeline, use Save project to keep your editable work, then Export to create an updated MP4 and Download to save that file in your chosen folder.

Understand the settings

Appearance, characters and video format
Visual style
Cinematic realism gives a film-like look; 3D animation / avatar uses a rendered character style; Anime uses a drawn animation style; Documentary favors a natural look; Product showcase favors clean product shots. Style guides appearance. The Video creation mode determines how scenes move.
Character preference
Follow my prompt uses the character described in your text. Realistic presenter requests a human appearance; 3D animated avatar requests a 3D character. These preferences do not enable talking or lip-sync. Choose Avatar explainer for a talking presenter.
Video format
Landscape 16:9 suits YouTube videos and presentations. Portrait 9:16 suits Shorts and Reels. Square 1:1 suits square social posts. This setting controls the frame shape.
Motion detail
Fast draft is for composition checks and may show visible artifacts; Balanced is the middle option; High detail uses more processing and takes longer. This setting applies to Hybrid and Full AI motion. Exporting at 1080p cannot add detail missing from the source.
Scripts, scenes, duration and online references
Scenes
Auto shots lets AI plan shots from your idea. Sentences makes one scene per sentence in a finished script. Paragraphs splits a script at blank lines. Manual tags uses [Scene 1], [Scene 2] and so on to mark each scene. Your scene count must fit the target duration.
Target duration
The requested video length. Actual timing may change to fit narration. Hybrid and Full AI motion support 10–60 seconds; Image story and Avatar explainer support 10–180 seconds. Longer videos may require more processing.
Online references
Sends your topic to Wikipedia to gather background information. To work from a finished script, turn references off and use Sentences, Paragraphs or Manual tags. Review the planned scenes before generating.
Voice, subtitles and presenter options
Narration language / Add narration
Choose the language for the voiceover. Turn Add narration on for generated speech, or off to omit it. AI talking avatar requires narration. Use Voice settings & preview to hear a sample; available voices depend on your setup.
Show subtitles
Displays generated scene text as captions in the video. This is separate from the narration toggle. For the actual spoken words in imported footage, use Transcribe speech.
Presenter / Color theme
Alex has a short-hair appearance; Maya has a long-hair appearance. Indigo studio and Teal studio set the slide colors. AI talking avatar generates its face automatically, so no photo upload is required.
Presenter layout
Side-by-side places the presenter and slides next to each other. Picture-in-picture Right or Left places a smaller presenter frame over that side of the slide.
What is the difference between Save, Export and Download?
Save project
Saves the editable timeline on this PC. Creating an MP4 is a separate step. Check the Saved/Unsaved indicator, and use Open projects to return to saved work.
Export
Renders your current edits into a new H.264 MP4 at 720p or 1080p, 30 fps. Wait for the export to finish.
Download MP4
Copies a completed video to your chosen folder. “Download previous export” means the file is an older version. Export again to include your latest edits.

What needs a connection?

Local editing

After setup, editing uploaded footage and exporting MP4 do not need Colab. First-time speech transcription may download its model.

AI generation

Keep Colab running and paste its current one-paste connection link. Images, motion and realistic presenters need their services ready. Direct neural narration needs Internet.
